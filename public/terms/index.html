A configuration check, not a booking guarantee.

Timezone Slot Proof is a free utility that projects configured availability with the timezone data available in your browser. Use the result as evidence to review—not as a promise that a separate service will behave identically.

Acceptable use

You may use, share, and export results for lawful personal or business purposes. Do not attempt to disrupt the service, introduce malicious files, or misrepresent an exported proof as an audit of a third-party scheduler.

No scheduler integration

The tool does not connect to Calendly, Cal.com, SavvyCal, or any calendar provider. Results do not account for busy events, date overrides, buffers, booking limits, a provider’s timezone database, or provider-specific recurrence logic. Verify important results in the actual booking page.
