Privacy without a calendar login.

Short version: your booking hours, client time zones, and imported calendar files are processed in your browser. The product does not upload them.

Data the tool uses

The check reads the booking hours you enter and any calendar file you choose. It processes them on your device. Calendar files are not uploaded.

Storage on your device

Your latest weekly configuration is saved in this browser so it can be restored. Imported calendar-file contents stay only in the open page.

Review links and exports

A review link contains weekly booking hours, client time zones, and dates. Calendar-file contents are never included. CSV exports are created in your browser.

Network and measurement

The sample check makes no third-party requests. The production host receives standard web request data in infrastructure logs.
